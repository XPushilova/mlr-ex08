# MLR Exercise 8

This app identifies apple, cherry, chestnut, and maple leaves from a camera or photo.

[Open the leaf classifier](https://xpushilova.github.io/mlr-ex08/)

On your phone, tap **Start camera**, allow camera access, and center a leaf in the square. The app shows a probability for each class. Tap **Choose photo** to use a saved image. The browser runs the model on your device.

Task 2 exports a trained MobileNetV3-Small model to ONNX and uses it in the app. Task 4 compares models and training changes on clean and camera-like validation images. The notebook contains the experiment results.
